# Rapsometeddy Business OS

A lightweight business management platform for small businesses and solo entrepreneurs.

## Vision

Business OS brings customers, products/services, sales, expenses, invoices and business analytics into one simple dashboard.

## Planned features

- 📊 Business dashboard
- 👥 Customer and lead management
- 🛍️ Products and services
- 💰 Sales tracking
- 💸 Expense tracking
- 🧾 Quotes and invoices
- 📈 Revenue and profit analytics
- 🔔 Follow-up reminders
- 🤖 AI-assisted business insights

## MVP roadmap

### Phase 1 — Foundation
- Dashboard shell
- Supabase database
- Business profile
- Customers
- Products/services
- Sales
- Expenses

### Phase 2 — Money tools
- Invoices
- Quotes
- Profit reports
- Monthly summaries

### Phase 3 — Growth tools
- Lead pipeline
- Follow-up reminders
- AI business insights
- Exportable reports

## Tech direction

Next.js + Supabase + Vercel, with a free-tier-first approach.
